    What MVP 3 proves
    
        
    An AI that DIRECTS a live football broadcast from several real cameras: it picks the shot, understands the pitch, follows the story and reacts, using only what has already happened.
    
      
    
      
    
    
    
    
      
    🎬 1. It cuts like a director
    
        
    Every camera gets a live "card" (ball seen? centred? players? picture usable?). The director keeps every shot ≥ 3 s, waits 0.6 s before switching, never airs a bad picture and keeps the 180° rule (no flipping to the other stand).
    
        
    
      
    🗺️ 2. It understands the pitch
    
        
    Calibrated cameras feed ONE pitch map: players from every camera merged, the ball tracked and remembered, both teams from their shirt colours, possession, ball into the box / out / over the goal line.
    
        
    
      
    🧠 3. It follows the story (MVP 2 inside)
    
        
    Gemini watches the match in short looks and writes what is happening, the phase and the story so far; the Colab action model (GPU) spots passes, shots, crosses every few seconds. Both feed the intensity curve and can trigger a reaction shot.
    
        
    
      
    🥅 4. It checks goals with its own eyes
    
        
    When the map says play is near a goal, the goal watcher asks Gemini to look at that goal's camera (sharp, high detail), then again at the aftermath. A goal only counts when the players line up for a kick-off afterwards.
    
        
    
      
    ⏱️ 5. Honest live: no peeking
    
        
    The clock is the match clock. Every AI answer is shown when it ARRIVED (+delay) and nothing reads frames that have not played yet. A run that falls behind real time is marked invalid. Ground truth (when a dataset has it) is only used for scoring, never for directing.
